Sonnenseite privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Applies to the Sonnenseite app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to the web version.

Sonnenseite has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Its maker runs no server and receives none of your data. What the app remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (starting point, saved places, filters, units, theme), the most recent forecast and the drive times it looked up, so it opens quickly and works for a while without a connection. This is kept in the app's local storage on your device only.

To remove it, open Settings in the app and choose Clear cached data or Reset all settings. Deleting the app removes everything.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap Use my position. It reads your position once at that moment. It does not track you, and it never reads your location in the background.

Your position is used to find the nearest known place and its height, to estimate drive times and to look up public transport connections. It is saved on your device as your starting point until you change it. If you prefer, choose a starting point by name instead and the app never asks for your location.

Services the app talks to

The app gets its data from the public services below. Each one sees your IP address and basic technical details of the request, as any website does. The app sends no names, accounts or advertising or device identifiers.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not sell or share data with advertisers.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting data

The maker holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Data on your device can be removed as described above.

Safety notice

Sunshine, fog height and travel times are estimates from forecasts and can be wrong. Conditions in the mountains change quickly. Before a trip check the official sources, such as MeteoSwiss, the avalanche bulletin of the SLF and the lift operator. Use the app at your own risk.
